# Stampboard: design

World: a dark departure board above two ruled ledgers on warm grid paper.

- Type: Archivo variable (condensed 70–85% widths for headings and figures), JetBrains Mono for data only; both self-hosted under `fonts/`. Tabular numerals everywhere.
- Colour: ink on paper; colour only marks state. Vermilion `--seal` is reserved for what needs attention; `--pass` green, `--run` blue, `--lamp` yellow only on the board. Sticky notes have their own manila `--note` surface; it marks the owner's private notes, not data.
- Board: running rows with a yellow lamp, queued rows after a "Queued" separator; self-hosted runners as cream plates with a hashed hue square, GitHub-hosted as outline plates; mono elapsed time.
- Ledgers: PR and issue lists, each row title + mono meta line (repo#number, author, branch/labels), state cells, seal, relative age. A 3px double rule under each head. When a ledger spans two or more repos, a one-line, horizontally scrolling Repo chip row sits under the head: paper chips (ink text, ink fill when pressed), single-select, independent of the board; the head count and summary stay unfiltered.
- Resize: every section head ends in a 28px Enlarge / Fold pair (44px hit). Fold leaves the head (a 50px bar for the board, a 56px spine for a ledger) and keeps its counts and seal notes. Enlarge doubles the section's area while the others shrink in proportion above their floors. The layout is stored in `stampboard-layout`. Reset layout sits in the footer. Resizing is instant.
- Notes: 240x216 manila notes (`--note`) with a 36px header (Move, title from the first line, Delete) and ruled text. They float in a fixed layer above every section and move by drag, by Move then click, or by arrow keys in 24px grid steps. New note and Gather notes sit at the right end of the footer. Gather piles every note in the bottom-right corner, headers 36px apart. Note text stays on this machine and is never logged or sent (`docs/design/sticky-notes.md` §5.1).
- Agents sheet: a full-width ledger between the board and the PR/issue ledgers, shown only while a Claude Code session runs or an ssh host is unreachable. Same head as the ledgers; sessions sit in hairline-ruled columns (name, mono host and cwd, Working/Idle state cell, age), every active subagent in a 1px-ruled nested list. Unreachable hosts are the sheet's only seal-red text. The head shows the total (sessions + subagents) and a muted `n leads · m subagents` split. While it shows, the board keeps 3, 4 or 5 job rows (below 920px, 920 to 1039px, and 1040px or more tall). The sheet yields first, down to a 104px floor, and scrolls under its `min(34vh, 360px)` cap. The ledgers keep a `clamp(150px, 55vh - 290px, 232px)` floor (+34px with chip rows). These rules hold until the owner resizes a section; the explicit layout (`docs/design/section-resize.md`) then replaces the row guarantee, the 34vh cap and the yield order with proportional shares above the same floors. In the flowing layout it caps at 75vh like the ledgers. Its body takes a tab stop only when it scrolls.
- Logo: two board bars and a tilted vermilion stamp on a rounded tile (`docs/design/landing/option-1`), beside the name in the board head and as the tab icon.
- Signature: the rotated double-bordered vermilion seal (CI BROKEN, CHANGES, APPROVED on pull requests; the configured `sealLabels` on issues). Its stamp animation is the only motion and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Language: Turkish when the browser prefers it, English otherwise, overridable with the EN/TR segment beside the theme switch and stored in `stampboard-lang`; every visible string lives in `STRINGS` in `index.html`.
- Themes: light and dark tokens on `:root`, auto by `prefers-color-scheme`, overridable and stored in `stampboard-theme`.
- Layout: fills 100vh at 1920x1080 with internal scrolling; below 1280px wide or 760px tall the page flows and the ledgers stack. Below that, Fold still works and Enlarge is hidden.
